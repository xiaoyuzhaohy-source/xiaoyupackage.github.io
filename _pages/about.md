## About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at **The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU)**. My research focuses on sustainable wastewater treatment, biological nitrogen removal, and resource recovery. In particular, I investigate **anaerobic ammonium oxidation (anammox)**, **partial nitrification (PN)**, **partial denitrification (PD)**, and integrated nitrogen-removal processes, with an emphasis on reactor performance, microbial mechanisms, and the mitigation of **nitrous oxide (N₂O)** emissions.

My research aims to develop efficient and low-carbon technologies for the treatment of high-strength wastewater and anaerobic digestate, while improving nutrient recovery and understanding the microbial processes underlying treatment performance.

## Research Interests

- **Biological nitrogen removal:** anammox, partial nitrification/anammox (PN/A), and partial denitrification/anammox (PD/A).
- **Integrated wastewater treatment:** coupling PN, anammox, and PD for nitrogen removal from high-strength wastewater and anaerobic digestate.
- **Granular sludge and mineral-assisted processes:** hydroxyapatite (HAP)-associated granules and expanded granular sludge bed (EGSB) reactors.
- **Greenhouse gas mitigation:** N₂O formation, transformation, and emission control in biological nitrogen-removal systems.
- **Resource recovery and microalgal processes:** nutrient recycling and interactions between humic substances and microalgae.

## Selected Research

### Integrated nitrogen removal from anaerobic digestate

Development and evaluation of PN-, anammox-, and PD-based treatment configurations for nitrogen-rich anaerobic digestate, with a focus on process integration and stable nitrogen removal.

### HAP-associated granular sludge systems

Investigation of HAP-strengthened granular sludge and EGSB reactors to enhance biomass retention, settleability, and nitrogen conversion in anammox and PD processes.

### Nitrous oxide mitigation

Investigation of operational and microbial factors influencing N₂O production and removal, with the goal of minimizing greenhouse gas emissions from biological nitrogen-removal processes.

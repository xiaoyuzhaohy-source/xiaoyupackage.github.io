# Research Overview

My research focuses on the development and optimization of sustainable biological wastewater treatment technologies, particularly anammox-based nitrogen removal processes.

I aim to improve treatment efficiency, understand microbial nitrogen transformation pathways, and mitigate greenhouse-gas emissions through an integrated approach combining reactor engineering, microbial ecology, and process optimization.

---

## 1. Anammox-Based Biological Nitrogen Removal

Anaerobic ammonium oxidation (anammox) provides an energy-efficient pathway for nitrogen removal by converting ammonium and nitrite directly into nitrogen gas under anoxic conditions.

My research focuses on the development and optimization of anammox-based treatment systems, including:

- Partial nitritation/anammox (PN/A)
- Partial denitrification/anammox (PD/A)
- Integrated biological nitrogen removal systems
- Treatment of nitrogen-rich wastewater and anaerobic digestate
- Operational stability and reactor performance optimization

**Keywords:** Anammox, PN/A, PD/A, Nitrogen Removal, Wastewater Treatment

## 2. Granular Sludge Engineering and Reactor Optimization

Granular sludge technologies offer advantages in biomass retention, settling performance, and treatment capacity.

My research investigates the application of hydroxyapatite-associated granular sludge in expanded granular sludge bed (EGSB) reactors.

Particular research interests include:

- Hydroxyapatite (HAP) formation and accumulation
- Biomass retention and granular sludge characteristics
- Reactor hydrodynamics and up-flow velocity
- Spatial distribution of microbial activities
- Optimization of anammox and PD/A reactor performance

**Keywords:** Granular Sludge, Hydroxyapatite, EGSB, Reactor Engineering

## 3. Nitrous Oxide Production and Mitigation

Nitrous oxide (N₂O) is a potent greenhouse gas associated with biological nitrogen transformation.

Understanding and reducing N₂O emissions is important for improving the environmental sustainability of wastewater treatment technologies.

My research addresses:

- N₂O formation during partial nitritation
- Effects of wastewater characteristics and operating conditions on N₂O emissions
- N₂O consumption in anammox-associated microbial communities
- Roles of electron donors and denitrifying microorganisms
- Strategies for mitigating N₂O emissions without compromising nitrogen removal performance

**Keywords:** N₂O, Greenhouse-Gas Mitigation, Partial Nitritation, Denitrification

## 4. Microbial Ecology and Nitrogen Cycling

Microbial community composition and functional activity play central roles in the stability and performance of biological wastewater treatment processes.

My research explores microbial nitrogen cycling and functional interactions using molecular and bioinformatic approaches.

Key interests include:

- Microbial community structure and dynamics
- Nitrogen-cycling functional genes
- Metagenomics and metatranscriptomics
- Functional interactions among anammox bacteria, nitrifiers, and denitrifiers
- Microbial mechanisms underlying nitrogen removal and N₂O transformation

**Keywords:** Microbial Ecology, Nitrogen Cycling, Metagenomics, Metatranscriptomics

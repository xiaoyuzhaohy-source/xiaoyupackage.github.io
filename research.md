## 1. Energy-efficient biological nitrogen removal

Developing and evaluating combined partial nitritation (PN), anaerobic ammonium oxidation (anammox), and partial denitrification (PD/A) processes for high-strength wastewater, including anaerobic digestate.

**Keywords:** PN/A, PD/A, nitrogen recovery, high-strength wastewater, process optimization.

## 2. Hydroxyapatite-strengthened granular sludge

Exploring hydroxyapatite (HAP) formation and granular-sludge properties to improve biomass retention, settleability, and performance in expanded granular sludge bed (EGSB) reactors.

**Keywords:** HAP, EGSB, granulation, reactor hydraulics, microbial ecology.

## 3. Nitrous oxide emissions and mitigation

Investigating N₂O production, emission, and reduction in biological nitrogen removal systems, with attention to operating conditions, electron donors, and microbial functional potential.

**Keywords:** N₂O, greenhouse gases, metagenomics, nosZ, denitrification.

## 4. Nutrient recovery and algal biotechnology

Examining microalgal responses to digestate-derived humic substances and the implications for wastewater valorization and sustainable biomass production.

**Keywords:** microalgae, humic acid, fulvic acid, anaerobic digestate.
